# Beaker Lab reel ideas

Purple skeleton beaker + green cat beaker. Every reel ends the usual way: zoom into a beaker's liquid, the game's
bubble transition (no level number), gameplay with the game's own sounds, end card.

## In production

- **Who ate the last candy?** (Halloween 2026, Guy picked Oct 30): empty candy bowl; spotlight on the cat
  (whistling, "Wasn't me"), a candy wrapper stuck to the cat's tail gives it away; the cat corks itself out of
  embarrassment while the skeleton laughs. Comment bait: "Tag the candy thief in your house".
  Script: `halloween-2026/render_last_candy.py`. ad108, scheduled Fri Oct 30, 2026, 5 PM ET.

## Backup
